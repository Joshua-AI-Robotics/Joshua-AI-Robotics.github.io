JOSHUA

Joint Open-Source Hub for Universal Automation

Project Joshua is an open-source robotics framework for building robots from reusable components. Built on ROS 2, it brings hardware drivers, control, perception, and AI into one configuration-driven stack.

Define your robot’s devices and pipelines in a single configuration, then reuse the same interfaces across different setups. Explore the catalog to see what is integrated, what has been tested, and under which conditions.

Use your AI assistant with Joshua MCP to discover hardware, prepare configurations, and validate your robot setup.

    Coverage uses dated vendor research plus current catalog additions, not a worldwide total. Generic families remain searchable without being counted again alongside exact models.
